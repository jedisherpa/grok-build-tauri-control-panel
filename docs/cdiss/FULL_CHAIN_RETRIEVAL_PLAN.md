## Research grounding

Keeping word/occurrence representations until query-document interaction is supported as an architectural direction by [ColBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832), whose learned token representations are distinct from this source-based prototype. Context-weighted lexical graph use is an established approach in [UKB](https://ixa2.si.ehu.eus/ukb/) and [Personalizing PageRank for Word Sense Disambiguation](https://aclanthology.org/E09-1005/). These sources motivate preserving structure and using explicit graph links; their retrieval/WSD performance does not transfer to our implementation without measurement. No new graph propagation weights or learned parser are introduced here.
